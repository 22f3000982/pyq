Practice data and browser storage

No student account is required. Your latest paper scores, bookmarks, preferences and cached lists are stored in your browser. Scratch notes and unsent responses may be kept in tab storage. Clearing this site's browser storage removes those local records; they do not sync between devices.

The server temporarily stores an attempt and its responses to save answers and calculate results. Active attempts expire after approximately 24 hours; submitted results expire after approximately one hour. Expired records are removed by periodic or request-triggered cleanup, so removal is not instantaneous. These are temporary sessions, not a permanent student history.

Cookies, administration and reports

Essential session cookies support temporary guest sessions, security and administrator login. Admin account details are stored on the server. Formatting reports store the question, submitted description and report status. Emailing us provides your email address and whatever you include; please avoid unnecessary personal information.

Usage statistics

We retain minimal exam/practice usage records: paper and course names, start and submission times, coarse device category and a pseudonymous browser identifier derived from a random first-party cookie (up to one year). Admin statistics count active browsers, not verified people. Clearing cookies or changing devices can change this count. Usage records remain after temporary answers expire. Analytics do not store names, email addresses, IP addresses, raw User-Agent strings, answers or fingerprints. Only administrators can view reports. Removing the cookie does not automatically delete existing pseudonymous records; contact us for privacy requests.

Hosting and external services

Render hosts the application, Supabase stores server data, and Cloudflare R2 serves paper and image assets. Requests to these services can expose your IP address and technical request information to them. Operational logs may contain request paths, times, IP addresses or error details. External links are governed by the destination's privacy policy.

Advertising

This version does not load advertising scripts. If Google AdSense is enabled later, Google and other advertising vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses and related identifiers to serve and measure advertisements. Google may personalise ads using visits to this and other websites.

Read how Google uses data and manage preferences at Google My Ad Center. Browser settings can restrict cookies. Where required, advertising will need an appropriate consent setup before activation. This policy will be updated when that setup changes.

Contact and requests

For privacy questions or deletion requests concerning data you submitted, email {{about.contact_email}}. Browser-only records can be removed through your browser's site-data settings. Do not send passwords or API keys.
